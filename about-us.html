About Us

Dedicated to Humanity & Social Change

Kanungo Janseva Sanstha is a registered social welfare organization committed to empowering communities, supporting underprivileged families, and preserving cultural values.

Who We Are

Founded in 2025, Kanungo Janseva Sanstha works across multiple social sectors including education, health, youth development, sports, and cultural preservation.

We actively support old age homes, orphanages, and underprivileged families while promoting leadership and unity among youth through community programs.
