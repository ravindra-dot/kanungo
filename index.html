Who We Are?

Dedicated to Humanity & Community Development

Kanungo Janseva Sanstha is a registered social welfare organization working towards community development, youth empowerment, education, sports, and cultural preservation.

REG NO : PALGHAR/0000343/2026

Together We Create Positive Change

Since 2025, Kanungo Janseva Sanstha has been committed to supporting old age homes, orphanages, underprivileged families, and community welfare programs.

We also promote youth development through sports, leadership activities, and cultural events that preserve Maharashtra's traditions while inspiring future generations.

OUR FOUNDATION

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Guided by compassion, integrity, and service, we strive to create meaningful change through community-driven initiatives.

Our Mission

To improve lives by supporting old age homes, orphanages, youth development, education, healthcare, and community welfare initiatives.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive society where every individual has access to opportunities, dignity, education, healthcare, and a better future.

Our Core Values

Compassion, integrity, transparency, teamwork, equality, and dedication remain at the heart of everything we do.
