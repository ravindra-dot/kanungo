Our Work

Projects, campaigns, and initiatives creating real impact in society

What We Do

Our organization actively works in education, social welfare, sports development, and cultural preservation. Every initiative is designed to uplift communities and support underprivileged families.

15 August

Celebrating Independence Day Through Service

Every year on 15th August, Kanungo Janseva Sanstha extends support to orphanages and old age homes across Vasai–Virar by donating books, food items, and essential supplies.

Through this initiative, we celebrate the spirit of independence by spreading care, compassion, and hope within our community.

Dussehra Celebration

Dussehra – Inspiring Young Minds

On the auspicious occasion of Dussehra, Kanungo Janseva Sanstha organizes a special educational support program for children from underprivileged families. School stationery, notebooks, pens, drawing materials, and other learning essentials are distributed to encourage students to continue their education with confidence.

Beyond providing educational resources, the initiative seeks to motivate children to dream big, work hard, and build a brighter future. By combining the festive spirit with meaningful social service, we strive to spread happiness while promoting the importance of education.

Cricket Tournament

Kanungo Boys Trophy Cricket Tournament

The Kanungo Boys Trophy Cricket Tournament is an annual sporting event organized every January under the banner of Kanungo Janseva Sanstha. The tournament provides a platform for young cricketers to showcase their talent, teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship in a healthy competitive environment.

Apart from promoting sports, the event also serves as an awareness campaign encouraging youth to stay away from substance abuse and adopt a healthy lifestyle. It brings together players, families, and the local community while inspiring young individuals to pursue fitness, discipline, and positive life choices.

Kanungo Boys Govinda Pathak

Celebrating Dahi Handi with Community Spirit

The Kanungo Boys Govinda Pathak proudly represents Kanungo Janseva Sanstha during the traditional Govinda Utsav in the Vasai–Virar region. This cultural initiative celebrates Maharashtra's rich heritage while promoting values such as teamwork, unity, courage, and dedication.

Through active participation in Dahi Handi celebrations, our volunteers work together with discipline and enthusiasm, preserving traditions that have been passed down through generations. The initiative encourages young people to remain connected to their cultural roots while fostering community spirit and mutual respect.

Community Welfare Initiatives

Making a Positive Impact in Society

Throughout the year, Kanungo Janseva Sanstha carries out various community welfare programs based on local needs. These include visits to old age homes, support for orphanages, distribution of essential supplies, awareness campaigns, and participation in social service activities.

Every initiative is driven by our mission to build a compassionate, inclusive, and responsible society where every individual has access to care, dignity, and opportunities for a better future. Through the collective efforts of our volunteers and supporters, we continue working towards creating a lasting positive impact in the community.
